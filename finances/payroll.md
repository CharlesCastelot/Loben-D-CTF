# Payroll

Minions are paid every other Friday in:

- 60% direct deposit
- 30% pizza coupons
- 10% mysterious tokens redeemable nowhere

## Tier System

| Tier   | Title             | Rate (per heist) |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Bronze | Apprentice Lurker | $40              |
| Silver | Henchperson       | $90              |
| Gold   | Senior Henchperson| $180             |
| Onyx   | Hat-Bearer        | classified       |

## Bonuses

- +$25 if you remember to lock the lair door
- +$50 if you do *not* let the dolphin in
- &minus;$100 if you accidentally let the dolphin in (we know it was you, Greg)
