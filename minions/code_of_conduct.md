# Minion Code of Conduct

1. No backstabbing on Wednesdays. Wednesdays are for trust falls.
2. Lab coats must be worn unzipped to a stylish degree.
3. Do not feed the lair shark. It's already had three interns this month.
4. Group laughter must conclude with all minions making eye contact at once.
5. The break room microwave is for evil reheating only. No fish.
6. If the council leader monologues, you must look impressed even on Mondays.
7. Whistling on the way to a heist is forbidden. It tips off the squirrels.
