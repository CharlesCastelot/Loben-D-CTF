# Minion Recruitment

## Where to Find Them

- Local LARP meetups (cheap, motivated, already in costume)
- The line outside that one bagel shop
- Any subreddit with the word "lore" in it
- Discord servers about haunted Roombas

## Screening Questions

1. Have you ever been bitten by a goose? (Pass: yes)
2. Can you keep a secret? (Pass: any answer except "depends")
3. Are you the dolphin? (Pass: no)

## Compensation

- Free hoodie (one size, fits no one)
- Pizza Tuesdays (only on the third Tuesday)
- Direct deposit handled via `finances/payroll.md`
