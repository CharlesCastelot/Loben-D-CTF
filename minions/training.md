# Minion Training Curriculum

## Week 1 :: Basics

- Cackle technique (medium volume, 2.5s sustain)
- Door-slamming for dramatic exits
- Refusing to answer questions properly

## Week 2 :: Field Work

- Carrying suspicious crates without dropping them
- Pretending the suspicious crate is a birthday cake
- Running away when someone says "police"

## Week 3 :: Advanced

- Polite henchman small talk during elevator rides
- Tying knots that aren't shoelace knots
- The Hat Ceremony (mandatory for tier promotions)

## Final Exam

Multiple choice, 40 questions. Open-book. Bring snacks.
