# Phase 2 :: Operation Sleepy Monday

**Objective:** Replace 60% of the world's coffee supply with decaf, undetected.

## Tactics

- Infiltrate three (3) coffee distribution warehouses.
- Swap labels overnight. Use the laminated forgeries from Crate B.
- Bribe the night guard with artisanal cheese (he prefers Gruyère).

## Expected Outcome

Global productivity drops 12%. Council seizes the resulting power vacuum.

## Do Not

- Touch the espresso reserve. That is for personal use only.
- Tell Marcus. Marcus tells *everyone*.
